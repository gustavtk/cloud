Drop the authentic 3ple Lift logo in this folder, named exactly:

  3ple-lift-logo.svg   (preferred)
  or
  3ple-lift-logo.png   (transparent background, at least 600 px wide)

Then run, from the editable-sources folder:

  node build/build.mjs

Every banner's logo layer is relinked to this file and all five PNGs are re-exported.
The logo is fitted inside a 260 x 56 px slot (left-aligned, aspect ratio preserved).
On the dark banner (04) it sits on a cream rounded chip, so the standard
dark-green logo stays legible.
